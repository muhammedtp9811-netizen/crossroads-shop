Delivery

Free shipping across India, delivered in about 5 days.

Your delivery timeline

  1. You place the order. Pay by the option shown at checkout: Cash on Delivery, UPI QR or online payment.
  2. We confirm it the same day. We check your items and the address you gave us.
  3. Dispatched within 24 hours. Monday to Saturday, excluding public holidays.
  4. Delivered within 5 days. Shipping is free across India.

Good to know

  • Shipping is free. The price you see at checkout is the final price.
  • We deliver to serviceable pin codes across India within 5 days.
  • Sale weeks, festivals and bad weather can affect dispatch. If your order is affected, we message you.
  • Payment options follow the store setting. UPI QR orders are payment pending until verified; we dispatch after confirmation.
  • Please double-check your phone number and full address with pin code before placing the order.

Tracking your order

Open Track order and enter your order number and phone number to see the latest status. We also send updates as the parcel moves. Need more help? Message us on Instagram or email with your order number.

If your parcel is late

Most parcels arrive within 5 days. If yours is late, message us with your order number and we will trace it and sort it out. You will never be asked to wait without an answer.

Wrong or incomplete address

If you notice a mistake after ordering, contact us straight away. If the parcel has not been dispatched, we can usually correct the address. If it has already gone out, we will help you arrange redelivery where the courier allows it.

Store policy

Clear, simple terms for shopping with Crossroads Shopping.

Last updated 24 November 2026

Orders and payment

The payment options shown at checkout follow the store setting: Cash on Delivery, UPI QR, or online payment by card/UPI/net banking. UPI QR orders are payment pending until verified. We begin packing the same day. Prices are in Indian Rupees and include everything: there are no hidden charges.

Pricing and shipping

Shipping is free on every order. The total shown at checkout is the amount you pay. If a price looks wrong on the site, message us before paying and we will correct it.

Delivery time

Orders are dispatched within 24 hours on working days and delivered within 5 days. Shipping is free across India. See the Delivery page for the full timeline.

Returns, exchanges and damaged items

  • Check your parcel as soon as it arrives.
  • If an item is damaged, missing, or not what you ordered, message us within 48 hours with a photo or short video of the item and the parcel. We will replace it, exchange it, or refund it.
  • To exchange for a different size, contact us within 48 hours. Exchanges depend on available stock, and the item must be unused, unwashed and have its original tags.
  • Please contact us before sending anything back. Returns sent without a message cannot be traced to your order.
  • For hygiene reasons we cannot accept returns on innerwear, cosmetics, or pierced jewellery unless the item arrived damaged or faulty.

Cancellations and refunds

You can cancel an order any time before it is dispatched. Message us with your order number and we will cancel it and refund you. Once an order has been dispatched it cannot be cancelled, but our damaged or wrong item policy still applies. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method, usually within 3 to 7 working days.

Product details

We describe every product as accurately as we can. Sizes follow the size chart on the product page. Colours can look slightly different from one screen to another. If you are unsure about a size or fit, message us before ordering and we will help you choose.

Privacy

We use your name, phone number, email and address only to process and deliver your order and to answer your messages. We do not sell your details. Payment information is handled by our payment provider; we never see or store your card or UPI PIN.

Contact us

For anything about an order, a return or a product, reach us here. Please include your order number so we can help faster.
