About Crossroads Shopping

Your next favourite find is here.

Crossroads Shopping is an online fashion store. We bring dresses, trending items, shoes and accessories together in one place and deliver them to your door.

What you can shop

Use the menu at the top of every page to browse each category, like Dresses, Trending and Shoes. New items are added regularly.

How ordering works

  1. Pick your product. Choose your size and add it to your cart.
  2. Pay securely. Pay by UPI or online payment and enter your delivery address.
  3. We confirm and ship. We check your order and send it to your door. Message us any time if you need help.
